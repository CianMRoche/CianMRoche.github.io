- Schneider, Ehlers & Falco (1992), *Gravitational Lenses*, Springer. Multiplane lensing (<a href="/caustica-documentation/multiplane/">§3</a>).
- Kormann, Schneider & Bartelmann (1994), Isothermal ellipsoidal mass distributions in gravitational lensing, A&A 284. SIE deflection (<a href="/caustica-documentation/lens-models/">§5</a>).
- [Tessore & Metcalf (2015)](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2015A%26A...580A..79T/abstract), The elliptical power law profile lens, A&A 580, A79. EPL deflection and potential (<a href="/caustica-documentation/lens-models/">§5</a>).
- [lenstronomy](https://github.com/lenstronomy/lenstronomy) (Birrer et al.), `LensModel/Profiles/epl_numba.py`. The code the EPL was ported from and checked against.
- Blandford & Narayan (1986), Fermat's principle, caustics, and the classification of gravitational lens images, ApJ 310. Arrival-time surface, image types and time delays (<a href="/caustica-documentation/lensing-quantities/#fermat-potential">§4</a>).
- [Orban de Xivry & Marshall (2009)](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2009MNRAS.399....2O/abstract), An atlas of predicted exotic gravitational lenses, MNRAS 399. Butterfly and swallowtail caustics; the Butterfly Caustic preset (<a href="/caustica-documentation/presets/">§11</a>).
- Lupton, Blanton, Fekete et al. (2004), Preparing Red-Green-Blue Images from CCD Data, PASP 116. The asinh brightness stretch (<a href="/caustica-documentation/source-models/#compositing-and-brightness-stretch">§6</a>).
- Smith & van der Walt (2015), the matplotlib viridis, inferno and plasma colormaps; Mikhailov (2019), the Turbo colormap. The map palettes (<a href="/caustica-documentation/lensing-quantities/#colour-scale-limits-and-palette">§4</a>), computed here from short polynomial fits.
