Assumptions

  • M = (4π/3) Δ ρref(z) R³, with ρc(z) = 3H(z)²/8πG and ρm(z) = Ωm(1+z)³ρc,0. No halo profile is assumed, so this cannot convert between different Δ for the same halo.
  • ΛCDM (w = −1); radiation and neutrinos neglected.
  • “vir” is the Bryan & Norman (1998) flat-ΛCDM fit.
